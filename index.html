Support

Quick answers to common questions.

Where should I record live TV?

If your BuzzTV device supports PVR, record to an external USB drive rather than the device's internal storage.

Should I install third-party apps?

We recommend avoiding unverified third-party apps. They can use storage, create stability issues, or introduce security risks.

How do I get help with my device?

Message Angie's Media Solutions on Facebook with your device model and a short description of what you're seeing.

Do you provide pricing on the website?

Pricing can change, so the easiest way to get current pricing is to message us directly.
